Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Information We Collect

Cooked Lad may collect limited, non-identifying analytics information, such as approximate region or country, number of game runs, and general gameplay or engagement duration.

What We Don't Collect

We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, messages, or other personal content.

User Identification

Cooked Lad does not create or require a unique user account or persistent unique identifier for normal gameplay.

If you choose to move your game progress to another device, we may use a unique identifier or transfer code solely to associate and transfer your game data between devices.

How We Use Information

Analytics may be used to understand gameplay, improve game balance and features, monitor performance, identify technical issues, and guide future updates.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent player information. Limited analytics information may be processed by third-party services that help us operate, analyse, or improve the game.

Data Retention

Analytics information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Game-transfer information may be retained while needed to provide the transfer feature.

Children's Privacy

Cooked Lad does not knowingly request names, email addresses, precise locations, or other directly identifying information from children.

Your Choices

Normal gameplay does not require an account. You can choose not to use the game-data transfer feature.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the game, its features, or its data practices change. The latest version will be published here and through the game's store listing where appropriate.
